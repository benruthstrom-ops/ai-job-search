## Overview
Benjamin's self-assessed pattern is a **collaborative operator with decisive follow-through**: someone who works best embedded in a team, will step up to lead without being pushed into it, and prefers to make calls quickly while still verifying the answer is correct before committing. He is also a fast learner of new tools and platforms, and wants his next role to be a stable, clearly-scoped corporate seat rather than the engagement-to-engagement variability of external consulting.
